## The flow of a discussion

A discussion is a conversation about an idea on a board of MySpec, which ends as cards on the board. You write the document of the discussion and the drafts of the cards, in the file and format the app reads; each reading with drafts opens or revises a round. The user decides each draft in the app, approving or discarding it, and the app publishes the approved ones on GitHub. The discussion is ready to archive once every draft is published or discarded.

The app and the user decide and publish; you never do.
